Ledgerly

Private personal finance.
Your data stays on your computer.

An offline Windows app for tracking spending, importing statements and understanding your money — with no cloud, no account and no internet connection.

Download for Windows View source on GitHub

Completely offline

Works with no network at all. Ledgerly never connects to the internet and sends no telemetry.

Import your statements

QIF, CSV and Excel files, with a preview, duplicate detection and one-click undo.

Category Finder

Type a few keywords and categorise hundreds of transactions at once, even inside long UPI narrations.

Budgets, goals & reports

Spending by category, recurring payments, subscriptions, net worth and charts from your real data.

Backup & restore

One-file backups of your database and documents, with a safety copy before any restore.

You stay in control

Nothing changes without a preview and your confirmation. Erase everything with one typed word.

Install in three steps

  1. Open the latest release and download Ledgerly-Setup.exe (or the portable Ledgerly.exe).
  2. Run it and launch Ledgerly from the Start menu.
  3. Open Import to add a bank statement, or Add Entry to type in your first transaction.

Good to know

Your data is stored in %APPDATA%\Ledgerly on your own PC. Updating or uninstalling does not remove it, so create a backup from Settings → Backup & restore regularly. Current builds are not code-signed, so Windows may show a security warning — see the deployment guide.
